# Attribution and source policy

## Primary course source

This roadmap follows the topic sequence of ETH Zurich's Spring 2026 course [Robot Learning: From Fundamentals to Foundation Models](https://cvg.ethz.ch/lectures/Robot-Learning/), taught by Oier Mees.

The ETH course page, lecture recordings, slide decks, papers, and official homework repository remain the property of their respective authors and publishers. Linking to a resource does not place it under this repository's license.

## What is included here

The study guides, SVG concept diagrams, discussion prompts, session plans, and build milestones in this repository are original SRA VJTI study-group material. They summarize general technical ideas and point readers to primary sources.

## What is intentionally not included

Access-controlled lecture decks are not copied, decrypted, or redistributed. Members with legitimate access should use the official course links. Do not commit passwords, downloaded decks, paper PDFs, or copied slide text to this repository.

## Citation practice

When adding material:

1. Link to the original paper, project page, lecture, or documentation.
2. Clearly distinguish a source's reported result from our own observation.
3. Prefer paraphrase and original diagrams over copied text or figures.
4. Record licenses before reusing code, datasets, or images.
